Rungnote

Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Rungnote does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics tools or servers of our own.

Learning data

Review schedules, mastery, diagnosis results and settings stay on this device. We cannot receive or see them. You can erase everything with “Clear learning data” in Settings, or by deleting the app.

Find a concept from a photo

Photos you take or pick are read for text on the device and then discarded. They are never stored or sent anywhere.

AI labels

Answers marked AI are generated on the device. Questions and photos are not sent to any server. They can be wrong, so please use the report button if something looks off.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you photograph a problem.
  • Photos: only for a photo you pick.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on review reminders, at most once a day.

Age

Rungnote is for ages 15 and up. Children under 14 need a guardian's consent.
